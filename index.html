02 · About

Clarity is the product.

I'm a product designer who works best where the problem isn't fully defined yet: competing constraints, dense information, high stakes. I care about interaction details, and just as much about the systems behind them: information architecture, design tokens, API-aware handoff and the decisions an interface enables.

I've led UX for a native Android app used in construction surveying, designed analytics and reporting SaaS, and built an AI-native design system. I work daily with product managers, engineers, QA and global client stakeholders.

I also build my own tools. UX Direct, a Figma plugin and AI design agent I'm developing, creates native Figma designs and checks them against objective quality criteria.
